# Dataset Annotation Guide

## 1. Overview
This guide defines how flaw spans are annotated and verified for evaluation of the Scoring Agent. All Tier 1 and Tier 2 audio mirrors must be annotated with precise spans matching the `GroundTruthSpan` schema.

## 2. Span Boundary Convention
- **Pauses**: A pause span includes the silence only. Do not include the trailing end of the previous word or the leading edge of the next word.
- **Pace (Rushed/Slow)**: The span encompasses the exact words that are affected.
- **Energy (Loud/Quiet)**: The span encompasses the words affected.

## 3. Review Protocol
- Algorithm-proposed spans (such as those generated by `make_tier1.py`) are initially labelled with `review_status: unreviewed`.
- A human must listen to the span. If the boundary is inaccurate, they adjust it.
- After review, change `review_status` to `reviewed`.
- Only `reviewed` spans are eligible for evaluation in `eval/`.

## 4. Provisional Label Tolerance
- Human reviewers cannot mark word boundaries with exact millisecond precision.
- **Provisional Tolerance**: +/- 50ms for start and end boundaries.
- The actual tolerance will be measured by having two reviewers independently annotate 20 samples (Dataset D13) and calculating their average deviation.
- Do not claim millisecond accuracy. Use 10ms (0.01s) rounding for storing timestamps.
